  - q: "Is CallPlan free?"
    a: "Yes, CallPlan is free on the App Store. Optional premium features extend the functionality."
  - q: "Does CallPlan work offline?"
    a: "Yes, the app works fully offline. Your data stays on your device – no forced cloud sync."
  - q: "How does CarPlay integration work?"
    a: "Once your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, CallPlan appears on the vehicle display. Dictate notes hands-free with voice commands."
  - q: "Which devices support CallPlan?"
    a: "iPhone with iOS 15 or later. CarPlay support requires a compatible vehicle."
  - q: "How are notes sent after a call?"
    a: "CallPlan automatically sends your notes via email or messenger – to yourself, your team, or directly to the customer."
  - q: "Is there an Android version?"
    a: "CallPlan is currently iOS only. Android and other platforms are planned."
