<section class="faq-section" aria-labelledby="faq-support-heading">
  <div class="section-header">
    <span class="section-tag">FAQ</span>
    <h2 id="faq-support-heading">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="faq-list">
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">Is CallPlan free?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Yes. CallPlan is free – with 10 planned calls to try it out. Premium unlocks unlimited calls, calendar sync, Apple Watch and CRM integration. <a href="/en/premium/">View pricing →</a></p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">How do I cancel my Premium subscription?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Open Settings on your iPhone → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Find CallPlan and cancel anytime.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">Does CallPlan work offline?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Yes, fully. All data stays on your device – no forced cloud sync.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">How does CarPlay work?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Once your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, CallPlan appears automatically on the vehicle display. Start calls and dictate notes – completely hands-free.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">How do I set up Siri Shortcuts?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Go to Settings → Siri &amp; Shortcuts in the app. You can create custom voice commands to create and manage calls.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">Which calendars are supported?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook. CallPlan automatically detects phone calls and ignores video calls.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">Which devices support CallPlan?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">iPhone with iOS 15 or later. CarPlay requires a compatible vehicle, Apple Watch requires watchOS 8 or later.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq-item">
      <summary class="faq-question">Is there an Android version?</summary>
      <p class="faq-answer">CallPlan is currently iOS only.</p>
    </details>
  </div>
</section>
